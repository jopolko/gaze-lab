Hold still and look at one spot: any wobble here is pure measurement noise. Moving your eyes should show as clear steps. If the noise is the same size as the steps, a game cannot see the move.

Face signals: which expressions can a game use as buttons?

Each bar is how far the signal rises above your own resting level (learned over the last few seconds); the white line is the level I treat as a deliberate press. Try: widen your eyes, squint, raise your brows, frown, open your mouth, smile. A signal that clears the line cleanly, without flickering when you do nothing, is reliable enough to use as a control on your face.

Physical limits right now

An eyeball rotates about its centre, so the iris shifts only ~0.21 mm per degree (12 mm radius). The iris is 11.7 mm wide, so pixels per degree = iris width in pixels x 0.0179. That number, versus the noise in pixels, is the accuracy ceiling no software can beat.
