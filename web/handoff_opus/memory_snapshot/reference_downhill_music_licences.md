In the game now (credits text is generated by `DhDirector.credits_text()` and shown in the MORE tab):
- **Alien Warfare Stems** by Kamihamiha, CC BY-NC 3.0, ccmixter.org/files/Kamihamiha/60882, 128 BPM, key A minor, 9:32, nine stems. Artist's note: remixes "pay what you like/free download at most", contact for anything commercial. Cut to 16-bar loops (windows chosen by loudness: ambience 136, pulse 32, bass 264, drums 88, piano 64, guitar 120, synth 248, acid 264, fx 232 bars).
- **Funk it!** by SunnyScy, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (freemusicarchive.org/music/sunnyscy/single/funk-it-1/). ShareAlike: don't edit; used unedited.
- **Funk Express** and **Quirky Funk** by butterflystudio, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (FMA). NoDerivatives: used unedited (format conversion + playback gain only). Pages say contact the artist about usage: TO DO ask permission (user wants to notify artists anyway; offer to draft notes, never send).

Everything in the game is therefore NON-COMMERCIAL (free release only). A paid/ads/store-monetised version needs permissions or replacement tracks. Commercial-safe candidates already evaluated (CC0/CC BY): Mechanichal Meaning of a Peanut (Calyman, CC BY 3.0, also has stems zip), 1 Minute DnB and Bouncer (Of Far Different Nature, CC-BY 4.0 / CC0, loop versions exist in Loop Box #2 / #4), Electric Exodus (FoxSynergy, CC-BY 3.0, "credit me as FoxSynergy"), Technological Messup and Space Echo (Centurion_of_war, CC0). Removed from the game by user choice 2026-11-17 (still in `gaze-lab-music-src/incoming`). Non-commercial others: Moodless Refrain (Apoxode BY-NC 4.0, 62 BPM), Drums Loop 65 (madamelabaronne BY-NC 3.0; planned heavy half-time layer for the peak), Blue Flames (Kara Square BY-NC 3.0, not downloaded).

Sound effects are procedural (AudioHooks), no third-party audio. Shapes of the six molecules are computed from formulas (credit line to Shulgin's PiHKAL).
