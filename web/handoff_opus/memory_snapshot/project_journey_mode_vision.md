On 2026-11-17 the user asked for an endless "journey" mode (also an "all dressed" menu option) that cycles through every world, course and effect: normal-looking play with rewarding flashes of trippiness that come in increasingly strong waves ("a normal person with an LCD trip coming on"). Requirements stated:
- Music (procedural techno, ambient, trance, jungle with amen breaks, industrial) changes and morphs with the environment and speed.
- Biomes differ noticeably in gameplay (speed, how reactive objects are) and objects behave slightly differently per biome.
- A super-hard or super-fast jump blasts the player above the clouds into the "flight" demo mechanics, with a full set of objectives and targets up there too.
- Difficulty, complexity and trippiness progress procedurally; it must work for a 30 second blast or a 6 hour session.
- Vertical control in flight: stay with head pitch (nod), eyes only for left/middle/right (the iris cannot be read vertically on a phone).

**Why:** the user wants variety and a sense of being led through everything, not one fixed world.
**How to apply:** build it in phases on the existing theme system (Themes.gd, ThemePost TRIP meter), the music engine (DhMusic styles) and DownhillRig/FlightRig; do not start it all at once. See [[gaze-lmr-control]] and [[psychedelic-look-loved]].

**Built 2026-11-17 (dev build 057e8e23, uncommitted):** `DhJourney.gd` (waves: calm alpine then a world washes in under a flash; strength 0.3 to 1.2, wave lengths 5 s growing to 75 s, difficulty grows without bound with clamps), per-world ride changes in `DhJourney.BIOME` (speed, rocks, ramps, pads, glints, bump/spring/pad strength, grip, music), music crossfade (`RunRig.music_morph`), menu switch "All dressed" (default on), `SkyRun.gd` (blast through cloud deck, flight controls with head nod + eye zones, three goals, aim assist, dive back), sky ramps and the fast-super-ramp trigger. Debug URL flags: `?jt=12` journey clock speed, `?jw=3` start wave, `?sky` force the sky at 6 s. Test: `tests/journey_probe.tscn` (env JT, SECS, SKY).
**Not yet verified:** on a phone, by ear, or for long sessions; per-world object visuals are unchanged (only numbers differ).

**Update (build 8b9cfd12):** the hard flash swap was replaced by a staged onset in `DhJourney` (CALM, ONSET, PEAK, OFFSET): the sky/mountains flicker in first, then ground, trees, lights, while the psychedelic pass spreads from the horizon (`onset` uniform in the POST shader). Calm stretches are 24 to 60+ s (x1.6 every fourth wave), first omen at 20 s. `ThemeFX.apply_part` applies one layer of a world. Full trip is reached from about wave 4. Tuning knobs are in `DhJourney._plan_wave/_calm_after`. Unmeasured: shader compile hitch on the first flicker of each world, and how it feels on a phone.
