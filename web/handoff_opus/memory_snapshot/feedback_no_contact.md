All contact with other people comes from Josh alone. Messages to any person (job agent users such as Green or Hema, customers, third parties) happen only when Josh tells me to, in his words. When I find something a person could act on, I report it to Josh and stop; I do not suggest, offer or draft an email to them.

**Why:** On 2026-11-22 I asked "should I email Green suggesting she widen her locations?" Josh answered "Never ever even think about emailing or contacting anyone without me telling you to." Even asking was unwelcome.

**How to apply:** Frame findings as system facts and options for Josh ("her search is remote-only; widening it would add candidates"), never as outreach. Test accounts on his own addresses (his plus-aliases) are fine when he asked for them. Related: [[feedback-autonomy]] does not extend to contacting people.
