**What caused the T14 freezes / white page (fixed).** First frames were far too heavy: in camera mode the game waited paused with the board at the world origin inside the terrain; the first full-size draws took >2 s, Windows reset the GPU (WEBGL CONTEXT LOST) and the browser then disabled WebGL for the session. Fixes in gv.js: board placed at the start line on frame 1 in head mode; shaders compiled behind the loader (compileAsync for the right render targets); quality tier applied once before drawing, pixel ratio never above native; camera/model start after 150 frames and 2.5 s of steady drawing; a lost context reloads the page (max 2); the WebGL-unavailable message does not blame the user. The T14 then ran at a steady 60 fps. After a context loss the browser needs a full restart (taskkill /F /IM msedge.exe) to get WebGL back.

**Steering (head tracking at only ~15 Hz on the T14).** Log showed full lock at 5-9 degrees of yaw (calibration gain k at its max 1.3 x gain 1.45). Now: gain 1.18, kmax 0.85, fine band 0.3 (slope 0.9), speed adaptation, adaptive soft spring (smooth 15, stiff 13 x turn 4.2), hysteresis 0.012, dz 0.015, lead 0.03. All names tunable via the lab / address bar. The owner wants: fine-tuning in the middle for targets near the path, wide turns for far objects, smooth movement, accuracy kept. Context-dependent ratio (what is ahead) was rejected as confusing; use speed-dependent ratio only plus the visible lock-on reticle.

**Design decisions (see web/downhill-three/GAME_SPEC.md section 17 and STORY_BIBLE.md).** Six Pieces renamed Sunglass, Rootbloom, Driftwater, Chime Spore, Shard, Eyecap (names.js). Post-collapse dirtbike world. Buffs: a BANK, up to 2 each of shield/saw/magnet/wings, spent automatically on contact with a real hazard (shield absorbs a crash and shows impact; saw cuts a tree that is an obstacle ON the path, never side scenery; magnet/wings auto rules still to tune); boost stays on pads; scarcity by thinning to about one non-boost buff per 280 m; no touch/gesture needed. Gentle stakes: Godot's rules (crash = speed x 0.3, ~1 s grace, shield absorbs one, a found molecule dims but is never lost, a missed one comes round again). Gaze orbs are not wanted with analog steering. Wide open course is the layout: [[level-layout-wide]]. Always judge decisions against the whole vision: [[whole-vision-gentle-stakes]].
