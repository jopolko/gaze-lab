When building a new game slice (The Last Copy, 2026-11-21) I left out two core pillars the owner had already set: the build-to-trip arc and molecules/senses (the Pieces) that you acquire and that unlock powers. The owner had to remind me each time.

**Why:** these are the identity of Downfall, not options. They are in web/docs/DOWNFALL_MASTER_PLAN.md section 4 (six Pieces: Sunglass, Rootbloom, Driftwater, Chime Spore, Shard, Eyecap; sense + power + what it un-redacts; powers persist across episodes) and in [[project_psychedelic_frozen_and_modes]].

**How to apply:** before designing or building any new level/mode/episode format, skim the master plan's pillars (Pieces, trip arc, redaction, face-steered, gentle stakes, plain words) and say how the new work carries each. Episode format must include a piece per leg and a trip curve.
