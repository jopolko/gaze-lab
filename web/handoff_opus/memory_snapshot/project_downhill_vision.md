On 2026-11-16 the user said the most compelling achievable gameplay is a Sonic-the-Hedgehog-on-the-ground / Sonic 3D style, based on simulated physics and momentum: a boost can carry the player into further boosts, of many kinds, so it is constantly surprising and pleasing. Very little failure ("just enough to keep it real"); it is meant to be an easy, relaxing game. Their son (Sonic-obsessed) found it relaxing and liked it; the goal is to tempt him with similar dynamics without ripping Sonic off.

Also decided: one look at a time, Alpine is the primary demo (other looks hidden unless `?looks`); readability on a phone matters more than flash (slower, fogged distance, clear colour code good vs bad, path obvious). Asked about adaptive difficulty from player measurements.

**Why:** phone is a small, fast-moving slice for the eye; relaxing + easy is the product.
**How to apply:** prefer chains of boosts/momentum over punishment; keep failure rare; do not add other looks or modes until Alpine reads well on the phone. See [[gaze-lmr-control]] and [[short-sequential-replies]].
