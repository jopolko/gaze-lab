Consider ALL of the project in its totality for every decision or suggestion. Do not propose generic or quick-fix solutions that fix one thing and bend the vision.

**The vision (2026-11-20):** a fast, relaxing, Sonic-like ride that builds toward a trip; the six Pieces (Sunglass, Rootbloom, Driftwater, Chime Spore, Shard, Eyecap; see web/docs/STORY_BIBLE.md) each add a layer of perception and the peak comes when all six are held; post-collapse dirtbike world where the Pieces are the next step of human evolution; three.js (light, instant, any laptop) at Godot-dev quality, which the owner loves; start with Godot parity, then extend. Buffs serve the hunt for the Pieces and protecting layers (magnet, wings, shield, saw), they are not a separate generic system; scarcity comes from the stakes.

**Penalties must be gentle:** no hard stop, no huge loss, but enough loss to make a player retry something a couple of times. Proposed: a crash is a stumble that drains about a quarter of the strongest layer (the picture dims), about 2 s grace, a missed Piece comes round again, layers only go out after repeated stumbles (about 3 in 30 s), a shield absorbs one, settled Pieces keep a floor of about half strength. Check what Godot's crash rule is before changing it.

**Why:** the owner said I was quick to "fuck up the vision with quick-fix bandaid solutions" and that I had designed buffs like a generic racing game.

**How to apply:** before suggesting anything, check it against the arc, the story, the performance bar and the forgiving tone; read the spec first (web/downhill-three/GAME_SPEC.md); say when something conflicts. Related: [[no-blame-user-hardware]] [[downhill-user-preferences]]
