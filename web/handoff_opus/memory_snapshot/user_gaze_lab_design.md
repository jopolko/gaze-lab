- Plays mostly on a phone, outdoors and in transit (streetcar, bus); control and camera frame rate matter more than screen frame rate; a cartoon on a small screen is forgiving, lost control is not.
- Agency: assists must not play the game for them ("my son will notice immediately if he is not really in control"); eyes choose, the board executes; wants instant tentative response that is confirmed or cancelled by further eye movement; far gates must be targetable.
- Privacy: diagnostics stay on the device, never record location (GPS speed only, opt-in), nothing is uploaded.
- Visual target: the Carve Snowboarding key art (hazy layered ranges, soft pink light, falling snow) plus a psychedelic mode for psychonauts and a candyland look; wants spectacular dynamic lighting and simple one-word pictograms.
- Wants: face callouts in the air (crazy eyes, tongue out) as a party game, passing the phone around; levels with their own mechanics, not just skins; procedural, endless, parameterised.
- Prefers short honest answers; asks for numbers (fps, run reports) rather than guesses.
