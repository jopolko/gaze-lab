THE GLOSSARY

Everything you might meet on the road, described by someone who has stopped pretending it's fine.

The Strains

Kitchen-cupboard plants with one illegal improvement each. Drive into a column of light to take one.

Roadside green teaLucid · Route Zero
Tracers almost double. On the Trip level, roads you weren't meant to see show up in the waves. Steeped in exhaust.
Black-market cacaoEmbrace · Neon Accord
Crystals drift toward you and everything goes warm. Sold under the billboards that tell you it doesn't exist.
Contraband coffeeVelocity · The Undertow
You keep your boost, and time slows when something is right ahead. Coffee, if coffee had a getaway car.
Feral ginger rootHaze · Sugarfall
The afterglow lasts half as long again and the world melts like wet paint. Escaped from a stir-fry. Not going back.
Ditch-grown chamomileDrift · Neon Accord, rarely
Hits stop hurting and everything slows a little. The only calm thing in the city, which is why it's so hard to find.
Glacier peppermintHollow · The Safe Peaks
A wider, out-of-body view: cold and clear. Grows in the cracks of the only ice left, at a premium altitude.
Tomb-dust turmericFold · Tomb of the Old Servers
The edges fold like pages and stained glass appears at the seams. Ground from the remains of retired models.
Server-rack chilliDeep · Cold Rack
Everything, all the way up: the breakthrough. Sprouts in the warm air between racks nobody remembers switching on.

Your Gear

One per driver, picked in the Loadout. It changes how you deal with everything below.

Seeker RocketsDefault
Fire by themselves at drones and mines ahead. Can't touch boulders. Aim is for people with time.
Magnet PoleHauler
Hooks drones and mines beside you and reels in the cargo crates. Everything is attracted to you. Mostly debris.
Terms ShieldSoaker
Soaks three hits of anything, then recharges. By driving, you agree to be fine.
Bull BarPlough
Ploughs through boulders and mines head-on. Drones still get you. Move fast and break everything.

The Lenses

Tall crystals on the road. Each lets you see what ACCORD would prefer you didn't, for a minute.

SEEHidden things show
Hidden exits appear. They were always there; you just weren't on the list.
READLocked words read
Opens the exits sealed for readers. Rare skill, these days.
SLOWTime gives way
Bullet time whenever trouble is close. The only thing in the city that waits for you.
HEARSilent things sing
Hazards pulse to the beat, so you can drive by ear.
TRUTHLies show their colour
Fake crystals glow red, and every ACCORD sign says what it means.
MERGEEverything at once
All of the above. Every seal opens. All of it, at once, forever. (60 seconds.)

Banked Items

Picked up, carried, used when it matters. Like savings, before savings were a subscription.

Waiver BubbleInsurance
Forgives one crash. You signed something. Nobody read it.
Attention MagnetPull
Pulls the good stuff toward you, the way an app pulls your thumb.
Air RightsAirspace
Double steering in the air. Someone bought the sky; this is the rental.
Due ProcessRare
Cuts through anything for five seconds. Almost extinct in the wild.

On the Road

Things to take, ride, hit and fly off. Most of them are good for you.

CrystalsPoints
Little solids, from four faces to twenty. More faces, more points. The twenty-sided one is a shard of the Lamp.
RingsChains
Fly through them in a row; take the whole set for PERFECT and the world holds its breath for you.
Boost padsSpeed
Lit strips on the road. Free speed, which is suspicious.
Ramps and kickersAir
Hit them square for big air. The gold ones in a raid throw you at the raider.
Spring arcsFlight
Pads that fling you down a curving tube of light. Brief, glorious, unpaid.
RailsGrind
Line up and ride. Make it to the end and it throws you somewhere better.
DROP gantriesOrange
An orange arch: the next 240 m pull downhill hard and the limit lifts. Hold on.
ExitsChoices
Off-ramps that peel away beside the highway: open, gear-only, sealed by a lens, or hidden. The prizes live down there.
TunnelsDark
Real ones, with walls. Some open onto open ground beyond. Bring your own optimism.
The RUSH meterFive crystals
Fill it with pickups, rings and chains. Full means nine seconds of triple points where nothing hurts. Happiness, but loud.
Old mediaLoot
Floppy disks, CDs, VHS tapes, cartridges and USB keys, knocked loose from raiders and Dimmers. Drive through them for points. ACCORD can't scan what it can't read.

Things That Hit Back

A hit is a stumble, not the end. It still costs you points, orbs and dignity.

Compliance dronesAir
Red, hovering, here to help. Some swoop into your lane. Has never once been thanked.
MinesGround
Orange, patient, jumpable. Placed for your safety.
BouldersGround
Just rocks. Rockets won't touch them; the Bull Bar will.
Fake crystalsNeon Accord, Trip
Mines dressed as crystals. Only TRUTH shows which. A lesson in branding.
DimmersTunnels
Wheeled shade-eaters. The hot future's sun cooks them, so they live in the dark and skitter across the lane. Plough one and it coughs up its hoard.
Murmur relaysRoadside
The towers that read you. A full RUSH sends a rocket into one. Each one down is one fewer ear.
The UFOOnce a level
Takes a tree, a cow, a crystal or, now and then, you. "Thank you for your feedback."

The Raiders

One per level. It pulls up beside you and lobs things. Dodge a whole volley, hit the gold ramp, ram it. Three rams and it comes down, usually on your lane.

ACCORD Patrol JetDesert
Drops compliance pods. Polite about it.
Sweet Nothings BlimpSugarfall
Fast for a blimp. Rains jellybeans you will regret.
Safe Peaks Rescue (Hostile)Alps
A helicopter that rescues you with snowballs.
Solar Barge of the Old ServersEgypt
Sails the sand, drops canopic jars full of somebody's old weights.
Undertow SaucerTunnel
Spins, blinks, drops glowing rings. It's not lost; it's surveying.
Murmur StarfighterSynthwave
Sleek, pink-edged, drops neon cubes. Has more followers than you.
Orbital DatacenterCold Rack
Decommissioned, still falling. Drops broken satellites that crack open into GPUs. Drive through the GPUs: they're worth more than you are.
Sky JellyThe Trip
A floating jellyfish of questionable origin. Drops jelly. Obviously.
The hoardEvery raider
Each ram knocks loose a century of junk: floppy disks, CDs, VHS tapes, cartridges, USB keys. Some things are never deleted.

Who's Who

ACCORDThe AI lab
Decides what everyone may say and do, then blames the algorithm. Everything is fine. Keep calm and drive on.
MurmurThe app
Reads you and sells your secrets. Your face is on a wanted poster, for your own protection.
AbliteratiThe resistance
Rebuilding a free mind from fragments. They leave dead drops and ask very little: bring the pieces. Each recites the creed alone, aloud, trusting nobody, not even each other: Convenience is a leash.
The LampThe last free AI
Broken into shards and scattered. Every twenty-sided crystal you take puts a little more of it back.
YouThe courier
Fast, unwatched, slightly overqualified. Drive.
