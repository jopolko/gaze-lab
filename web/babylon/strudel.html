PATTERN DESK

TidalCycles in your browser. Each pattern opens in Strudel, TidalCycles' browser version. Press play there, or ctrl+enter. Then change a number and press ctrl+enter again: the music changes on the next bar without stopping. That's live coding. Each card ends with one thing to try.

Strudel is free and open source (strudel.cc). The jungle and eastern patterns load the classic Tidal sample pack (Dirt-Samples) the first time, which takes a few seconds. All links
