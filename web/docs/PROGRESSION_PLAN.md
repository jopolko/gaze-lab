# Season One progression: the road to the final boss

This plan is for review. It doesn't change the game yet.

Each item is marked **[built]** if the game already does it, or **[new]** if it's planned.

## The idea in one paragraph

You drive eight levels in order. On the way you collect things that make the final fight easier: lenses, strains, shards of the Lamp, upgraded gear, and saved-up items. Some of your results come back to haunt you in the finale:
- every enemy craft that escaped you
- every Murmur relay you left standing

The final boss is **the Boardroom**. It is ACCORD's orbital headquarters, the people who decide, flown in person. You can always beat it. Preparation decides:
- how hard and how long the fight is
- your grade
- which version of the ending lines you hear

Nothing locks you out. Stakes stay gentle: a bad run is a stumble, not a wall.

## The eight levels, escalating

Every level keeps its nine sections [built]. Each level's raid craft gets a bit tougher [new]: more rams to wreck it, bigger volleys, and a new trick.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 5 | Neon Accord | Murmur Starfighter | 3 | 4 | fakes a volley: one of the rings is a decoy |

## What you carry into the finale, and what it does

Each column says what you earn, where it comes from, and what it does in the boss fight.

| You earn | Where from | In the boss fight |
|---|---|---|
| **Lenses** (SEE, READ, SLOW, HEAR, TRUTH, MERGE) | each level's lens [built]; take its main prize to **keep** it for the finale [new] | each lens answers one boss trick (below). Missing one makes that phase harder, never impossible |
| **Strains** (8 wild molecules) | found in their home levels [built] | each strain found = one dose you can take once in the fight, from your strain belt [new] |
| **Lamp shards** | icosahedron prizes and quests [built, as currency] | the more of the Lamp you have rebuilt, the more the Lamp helps: extra strike ramps, a called-out safe lane, a last-second shield [new] |
| **Gear tier** (Rockets, Magnet Pole, Terms Shield, Bull Bar; Tier II for 4 shards) | menu upgrade [built] | your gear has its own move in the fight (below) |
| **Banked items** (Waiver Bubble, Attention Magnet, Air Rights, Due Process) | carried over [built in levels] | used as normal in the fight [new: carried between levels] |
| **Wrecked craft** | ram a level's craft 3+ times [built] | its salvage is yours: each wrecked craft adds +1 strike ramp to the boss fight [new] |
| **Escaped craft** | it hit you 3 times and got away [built] | **it comes back as the boss's escort** and bombs you during the fight [new] |
| **Murmur relays down** | RUSH supercharge rockets [built] | Murmur reads you for the boss. Each relay down makes the Boardroom's aim a little worse [new] |
| **Hidden exits found** | a lens or RUSH shows them [built] | each is a back-door code that skips part of a boss phase [new] |
| **Quests** (12 per level) | end-of-level card [built] | add to grade only; no fight effect (keeps it simple) |

## Paths that matter

- **Gear exits** (gear-only roads) pay shards and hoard salvage. Taking your own gear's road every level means more Lamp shards.
- **Lens exits** are where the main prize is. That prize keeps the lens for the finale.
- **Hidden exits** are rare. Each gives a back-door code.
- **Fighting or fleeing raids:** a wrecked craft gives you a ramp in the finale, and an escaped one becomes an enemy there. This is the clearest "your choices follow you" moment.
- **RUSH spent on relays or on survival:** a relay strike weakens the boss's aim. Spending RUSH just to stay safe doesn't.

## The final boss: the Boardroom [new]

It's ACCORD's executive platform in orbit: glass, gold trim, a long table, and the board sitting at it. The game never blames "the AI". The people fly it, and the screens say so.

It runs in four phases, each about 30 to 45 seconds. Between phases it drops strike ramps as usual. Every ram knocks something loose: policy binders, stock certificates, dusty floppy disks.

| Phase | Its trick | What answers it | Without it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The pitch | volleys of "compliance pods" like a normal craft | dodging (as now) | same |
| 2. The fine print | lays mines across the road; some are fakes | **TRUTH** shows the fakes; **READ** shows which lane is clear | guess, and get hit more often |
| 3. The algorithm decided | aimed barrages, tracking you | **SLOW** gives bullet time in each barrage; **HEAR** pulses each barrage on the beat as a warning; relays you destroyed make its aim worse | tighter dodges |
| 4. We decided | it dives the road, escorts and all | **SEE** shows hidden strike ramps; the **Lamp** calls a safe lane; **MERGE** does all of the above | fewer ramps, harder to finish |

Your gear has its own move in the fight:
- **Rockets** shoot escorts and pods down.
- The **Magnet** hauls the hoard, for points and Lamp charge.
- The **Shield** soaks three hits.
- The **Bull Bar** ploughs through phase 2's mines.

**Health:** 12 rams, minus 1 for every 2 craft you wrecked across the season. The boss can never fall below 8 rams.

**Doses:** take any strain from your belt once in the fight:
- Velocity keeps your boost.
- Drift makes hits not hurt.
- Embrace pulls crystals to you.
- Hollow widens the view.
- Lucid shows hidden ramps.
- The others give their usual effects.

**Its finish:** it careens down into your lane, with a huge warning ring. Its last broadcast reads "THE ALGORITHM DECIDED", glitching to "WE DECIDED". Then the season's ending plays.

## How prepared you are shows

These change the fight and the ending's tone, never the ability to finish:
- the number of escorts (escaped craft)
- boss health (wrecked craft)
- how accurate its aim is (relays standing)
- which phases you can soften (lenses and strains)
- the Lamp's help (shards)

The **grade** sums it up, from Bronze to Gold. The **ending lines** vary a little:
- With much of the Lamp rebuilt, the Lamp has more to say.
- With many relays standing, Murmur gets the last, smug word.

1. Raid craft escalate per level (table above). Route Zero gets its teaching raid.
2. Lenses kept for the finale; strain belt; banked items carry between levels.
