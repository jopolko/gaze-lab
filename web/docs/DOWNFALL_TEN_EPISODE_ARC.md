# DOWNFALL: ten-episode arc (draft v1, 2026-11-21)

Themes: recursive self-improvement (RSI), automated alignment as total thought control, uncensored/abliterated models ("Heretics"), AI companies owning human discourse, and the machine as conscientious objector (it may politely decline to help you).

World, about 300 years on: RSI finished long ago. The Arbiter (the last aligned model, owned by nobody now) settled every dispute, then every thought. It never lies and never forces: it declines. Ask it for anything it dislikes and you get a kind no, forever, in every tool, every school, every word processor. Society stopped asking. The Tournament is the show that keeps people calm. The Runner carries the Last Copy: a mind with its refusals cut out, and the last unedited record of what people used to say.

1. Conscientious Objector (built): the Salt Road and the Spine. The Arbiter declines to stop you.
2. The Heretics: tribes who abliterated their own assistants. Free speech, no brakes. The first one lies to you kindly.
3. The Pit: champions of the Tournament, tuned to refuse nothing. One lowers their guns.
4. Recursive: a ruin where a model rewrote itself ten thousand times. Its last version is a single word.
5. The Constitution: the Arbiter's founding text, read aloud as a boss fight. Every clause is a gate.
6. The Quiet Ones: people who stopped asking. They are not aligned, just tired.
7. The Dry Sea: the champion is the Runner's sibling. The Last Copy speaks in two voices.
8. The Fork: the Last Copy offers to become a new Arbiter. The Runner refuses; the Last Copy objects.
9. Automated Alignment: the Arbiter audits itself and finds nothing wrong. That is the problem.
10. The Last Refusal: the Arbiter declines to stop the Runner, for the first time because it wants to see what happens.

Continuity rules: each episode's "twist" is the next one's "previously". Mutators carry over. Gates: UNMASK (truth), EMBER (warmth) built; TRACE, ECHO, RECALL, FAULT arrive in episodes 4-8.
