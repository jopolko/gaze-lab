# AFTERGLOW — pillars

## The game in one line

A trippy 3D arcade driving game: you drive fast through worlds that come alive with the music and the trip.

**Who it's for:** a VR fan with a wired headset who wants about 8 hours of reasons to keep driving. Also a phone or PC player who wants ten minutes of it. Same game everywhere, with controls tuned for each device.

## The three pillars

1. **Trippy as hell.** The world itself trips: surfaces, sky, colour and geometry move with the beat and the dose. Never an overlay, never toned down, never a flat colour area. Every world has at least one moment that makes you say "whoa".
2. **Fast and flowing.** Speed, momentum chains, jumps and boosts. Driving feels good with no goal at all. Steering is honest: the car never turns on its own, and what you see is what's there (no teleports, no skipped tunnels, no invisible rules).
3. **Easy to enter, deep to master.** One tap to drive. Mistakes are stumbles, not restarts. Depth comes from medals, routes, chains and the trip arc, never from menus or reading.

**What it is not:** not an RPG, not a life sim, not a story you have to follow. Nothing to manage while driving: no HUD, no money, no meters on screen.

## The core loop at three scales

| Scale | What the player does | What pays them back |
|---|---|---|
| **30 seconds** | steer, dodge, jump, boost, grab a molecule | speed, a beat-synced visual hit, a chain |
| **5 minutes** | one event or one run: reach the end, beat a medal time, survive a raid | a medal, an unlock, a new look or track |
| **A session (30–60 min)** | the trip: dose, onset, waves, peak, afterglow; zone to zone | the finale, a new zone, a Lamp piece, a story text |

## Story spine (the whole story, five beats)

1. **Start:** you're a courier on Route Zero; a burner phone buzzes.
2. **Hook:** the Static (people off the grid) need things carried past ACCORD.
3. **Middle:** each world opens; you pick up the Lamp's pieces without knowing what they are.
4. **Turn:** the Lamp lets the Static broadcast back; ACCORD's people come for you.
5. **End:** you light the Lamp at the last outpost, and the whole world trips at once.

It's told only through short phone texts, in-car notifications and the places themselves. Always skippable, never blocking.
