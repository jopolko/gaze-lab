# AFTERGLOW — cast, scenes and missions bank

*Electronic Anesthesia presents. Draft 1, 2026-11-26.*

The vibe: an open, hostile, sci-fi Earth you drive across like a crime-sandbox game (jobs from shady people, heat that builds, places that remember you), cut like a 90s outlaw movie. The touchstones are **Natural Born Killers** (media circus, outlaw as celebrity, channel-flip madness), **Pulp Fiction** (chapters, a briefcase nobody explains, tiny choices that kill people), **Mad Max** (convoys, scarcity, warlords, arenas) and **Terminator** (the thing that never stops coming, a future that wants you erased).

Rules every entry below keeps:
- **Our own names and scenes.** The films are inspiration for *situations*, never copies of characters or shots.
- **The villains are people.** ACCORD is a company of humans who build their interests into the AI and blame the algorithm. Machines are their tools.
- **Clean screen.** Every objective is shown in the world: signs, lights, the radio, the car itself. No HUD lists.
- **It plays with what the game is**: head/touch steering, momentum, exits, set pieces, raiders, gear (rockets, magnet, shield, bull bar), lenses, strains, RUSH. A mission that needs a new system says so.
- **The trip is the point.** Story is flavour you drive through, never homework.

## 2. Episode spine (one line each)

1. **Route Zero** (desert) — Diner breakout, first Lamp piece, scorpion drones, first raider, tunnel. Ends at Maro's door with the case.
2. **The Hunt** (alpine) — Ransom hunts you live on *ACCORD Live*. Boss: his gunship.
3. **Salt and Fuel** (salt flats / refinery) — Mama Tallow's convoy war. The Tournament arena first appears.
4. **The Comfort Belt** (candy) — the sitcom-world glitch; the sanitized broadcast town.
5. **The Undertow** (tunnels) — the server raid; Warden Pell's first checkpoints.
6. **Neon Accord** (synthwave city) — Vance's studio; you crash the live show.
7. **The Citadel** — the final break-in; the Closer's last rebuild; the Lamp goes live.

## 3. Scenes and missions

Each: **name** — the situation; *how it plays*; consequence.

### From the outlaw-media movie (NBK)
1. **The Diner Breakout** — A neon service station at night. Dex & Lolo are robbing it as you pull in for the first Lamp piece hidden in the jukebox. *Short on-foot-free scene: it's all seen from the car (lights, shouts, a shotgun flash); you leave at full speed as scorpion drones pour out of the dark.* The station owner remembers whether you left the robbers to it.
2. **Live on Air** — Vance's drones film your run; a ratings bar is painted on billboards you pass. *Do something spectacular (set piece, RUSH chain) under a drone and ratings spike; ACCORD sends heavier raiders the higher it climbs.* Fame is a resource and a curse.
3. **Channel Surfing** — A strain that makes the world cut like TV: every few seconds the scene flips (black-and-white, cartoon, night vision) while the road keeps going. *Steer through cuts; pickups only show in one channel.*
4. **The Interview** — Vance offers a live interview: pull into his roadside studio set. *Choose an exit: the studio (he gets his show, you get a fat bounty reduction) or the back road (he cuts to commercial and sends Ransom).*
5. **Wanted Posters** — Murmur puts your poster on every screen. *Drive through the poster gantries to tear them down for points; leave them up and civilians call in your position.*
6. **The Prison Break Broadcast** — Late game: Vance is live inside the Citadel when it falls. *You chase his broadcast van through the riot; ram it and his camera feed shows the world the truth.*

### From the chapter-crime movie (Pulp Fiction)
7. **The Case** — Maro's job: carry a locked case that glows gold when opened. Never explained. *Delivery run where the case glows brighter with every risk you take (set pieces, near-misses); arrive glowing and the pay doubles; crash and it dims.* What's inside becomes a fan theory, never answered.
8. **Going Back for the Watch** — You're clear of the chase, then remember: your father's watch (the one keepsake) is still in the motel you fled. *A real exit choice: keep driving to safety, or take the exit back into the heat. Going back is the harder road (raiders, the Closer waiting) but it's the only way to get the Watch, a gear slot that slows time briefly once per run.*
9. **Wrong Time to Stop** — A side character in your convoy pulls over for a break at exactly the wrong moment and is caught. *If you set the convoy's pace too hot, someone has to stop; that stop gets ambushed. Momentum choices matter.* (The off-guard death, played as a lesson, not a joke.)
10. **The Cleaner** — A job goes wrong (a bomb hit the case car). The Cleaner calls: 60 seconds to get the car somewhere safe and clean. *A timed run to a scrapyard exit; the car's damage shows on the bodywork, not on screen.*
11. **Out of Order** — An episode told in chapters out of order: you play the ending first (escaping), then the morning that caused it. *Same road, different time of day; the second time you know where the trap is.*
12. **The Dance Contest** — Maro's club, a dance contest — for cars. *A rhythm set piece: steer to the beat on a neon floor; the music engine already knows the bars.* Win a prize that makes no sense and matters later.
13. **The Spiral** — Two strains stacked send the world into a spiral; *one exit leads to a roadside shrine where the colours settle. Reach it before the screen whites out and you come down gently.* (Stays art, never a health message.)
14. **The Junk Market** — You and the rival chasing you both crash into a backroads junk market run by a crew that wants both cars. *Leave him there, or drive back in and pull him out. Save him and he owes you; he shows up in the finale.*

### From the wasteland movies (Mad Max)
15. **The Tanker Run** — Drive a fuel tanker through raider country. *Heavy car, slow turns, raiders board from the sides: ram them off with the bull bar; the tanker's health is the cargo sloshing on screen.*
16. **The Convoy** — Lead Mama Tallow's convoy of six trucks. *Choose the route by exits; every truck that arrives is fuel for the next episode.* Lost trucks are visible as burning wrecks next time you pass.
17. **The Sandstorm** — A storm wall rolls across the salt. *Visibility drops to almost nothing; only the sound and the rumble strips tell you where the road is. Head-steer by ear.*
18. **Polecats** — Raiders on swinging poles snatch at your roof. *Swerve at the moment they swing; the magnet pulls their poles off.*
19. **The Arena** — The Tournament: two cars enter, one drives out, the crowd watching on Vance's network. *The Smash Derby bowl as story: rules painted on the floor, a chant, a referee drone.* **[derby prototype exists]**
20. **Bridge to Nowhere** — A highway that ends in the air over a canyon. *The jump is a set piece; the only safe landing needs wings or full RUSH.* A dead end that becomes a shortcut later.
21. **Water Is Life** — A drought town. *Carry a water tank on the roof; every jump spills some. Deliver it full and the town becomes a safe stop.*
22. **The War Rig Chase** — The long one: a 10-minute running battle down one canyon, raiders on every side, a storm behind, a gate ahead. *Built from chained set pieces with no breaks; the episode's climax.*
23. **The Feral Kid** — Leave food at a burned-out shack three times and the kid starts following your car with a boomerang drone that knocks down scorpion drones.
24. **Bartering** — No shards in the badlands; trade the junk you collected for parts at a market of scrap stalls. *Exits are stalls.*

### From the machine-hunter movies (Terminator)
25. **The Closer** — An armoured chassis assigned to you. *It appears in the mirror (the world, not a HUD) and never stops: you can't outrun it on straight road, only through tunnels, exits and set pieces it can't take.* Destroying it costs ACCORD money; it's rebuilt bigger, with the damage welded over.
26. **Come With Me If You Want to Drive** — A stranger pulls alongside: "They've already predicted your route. Follow me." *Trust them (take their exit) or not. The predicted route is shown as a ghost car ahead of you: ACCORD's model of you. Beat your ghost to break the prediction.* (This is the master plan's "Predicted You".)
27. **The Liquid Car** — A pursuer that copies your car exactly, paint and all, then tries to bait civilians into reporting the wrong one. *Find the fake by its tells (it drives too perfectly).*
28. **Judgment Day** — The Citadel's network about to lock every car on Earth to ACCORD's routes. *A countdown shown on every billboard; the finale's clock.*
29. **The Factory** — A ride through an automated car plant where Closers are built. *Set pieces of presses and conveyor rails; smash the line to stop the next one.*
30. **I'll Be Back** — After its defeat, the Closer's head (a sensor pod) crawls after you on its own. *Comic, gritty: it shows up in later episodes on the side of the road, watching.*
31. **The Dam** — The steel works/dam finale: molten light, bridges that fall behind you. *The Closer's last chase on a collapsing route.*

### Original Afterglow situations
32. **The Comedown Town** — A town where everyone is still on yesterday's strain. *The world is slow, soft, washed out; take the right strain to sync with them and they help you.*
33. **Ghost Road** — A road that only exists while a lens is on. *Hidden exits, a ghost convoy, a reward that stays when the lens wears off.*
34. **The Lighthouse** — The Abliterati's lighthouse in the desert night: a calm stage, no enemies, the beam sweeping. *A breather mission: drive the beam's path to restore a Lamp piece.*
35. **Free Signal** — WREN broadcasts a burst of uncensored data across a valley. *For 30 seconds every ACCORD sign glitches to the truth, and drones lose you. Use the window to get through a checkpoint.*
36. **The Checkpoint** — Warden Pell's toll gates. *Lanes: ACCORD lane (slow, scanned, safe), cash lane (needs shards), and the barrier (bull bar or rockets). Your choice feeds your heat.*
37. **Heat** — A heat level painted in the sky (searchlights, more drones) that rises with what you do and falls when you hide in tunnels or change paint at a garage exit. The game's "wanted" system, shown in the world.
38. **Paint Shop** — A garage exit that changes your paint and drops your heat — at a price.
39. **The Recall** — ACCORD remotely "recalls" your car model: the engine starts cutting out. *Reach a pirate mechanic before you stall; your steering gets heavier as the countdown runs.*
40. **Night Drive** — No headlights allowed (they'd give you away). *Steer by the moon, the lightning, and the glow of strains.*
41. **The Wedding Convoy** — A wedding party's convoy on the same road as a raider attack. *Protect them and they throw you the bouquet (a one-run buff).*
42. **Last Exit** — At the very end of the game, one exit off the final road is unmarked. Take it and the credits play while you keep driving into the afterglow, forever, the music never stopping.
